Dartlog — Support

Dartlog is a darts scorer for iPhone and iPad: 501, 301 and Cricket for two to four players, with a tap-the-board input, live checkout suggestions, and tap-to-log scoreboards for playing on a real board.

Questions, problems, or feature requests — email j.crim@me.com and include your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a bug.

All match data is stored on your device only; see the privacy policy.
